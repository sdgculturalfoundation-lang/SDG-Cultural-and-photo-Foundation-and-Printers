Why culture and tourism education matter

Communities that know their own story welcome visitors with confidence. We turn that story into books, images and spaces that students, travellers and families can use.

01

Discover

We research the dress, crafts and landmarks of Plateau State and the wider region.

02

Preserve

We record what we find in photographs and publish it in textbooks and digital editions.

03

Share

We bring it to classrooms, events and visitors through our books and facilities.

Frequently asked questions

How do I buy a soft copy?

Pay online with a Visa, Mastercard or Verve card, then download the book from My Library.

Where can I buy a printed copy?

Visit our Where to Buy page for sales points, or order online.

Can I book the hall or grounds?

Yes. Send an enquiry with your date and event type and we will confirm availability.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Verve, bank transfer and USSD through Paystack.

Do you run workshops?

Yes, in tourism, culture and photography. Ask us for the next dates.
